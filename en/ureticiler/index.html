Growers

Our growers

Where every item on the menu comes from (fictional examples).

  • HÇ

    Hasan Çiftliği (kurgusal)

    Olives and olive oil

    Forty decares of olive groves in Nizip; cold-pressed oil and table olives.

  • AB

    Ayşe Bostanı (kurgusal)

    Seasonal vegetables

    Harvest twice a week; the menu is written around what arrives that day.

  • KD

    Kale Değirmeni (kurgusal)

    Flour and bulgur

    Stone-ground flour; the source of our lahmacun and bread dough.

  • FK

    Fıstık Kooperatifi (kurgusal)

    Pistachio

    The single source of the pistachio in our baklava and kunefe; declared with the tree-nut allergen code.
