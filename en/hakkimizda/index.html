About us

A menu written by the field
Zeytindali is a fictional demo restaurant inside an olive garden in Nizip that cooks whatever the season brings. The menu is rewritten twice a week around what arrives from the growers; every item lists its grower and allergen codes, and prices are final.
Named growers
Next to every plate you will find the farm it came from.
Clear information
Allergen codes and final prices are printed on the menu.

From field to table
